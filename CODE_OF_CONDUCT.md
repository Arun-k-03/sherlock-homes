# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Sherlock Homes a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and precise in security discussions
- Do not share exploit payloads against unauthorized systems
- Do not publish other people's secrets or production dumps
